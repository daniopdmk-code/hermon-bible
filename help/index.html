Support

A question, a failure, something to report: write to us. A real person reads, and answers.

Write to us: daniopdmk@icloud.com

Tell us your device (iPhone or iPad) and, if you can, what you were doing just before. Two lines of context save two days.

Reporting content or a person

In L’Assemblée, every post, every comment and every profile carries a menu with Report. The team reads every report within twenty-four hours: offending content is removed, and its author may be suspended.

You can also block someone at any time — their posts disappear from your view, and their comments under yours — and undo that block from the community settings.

If something strikes you as serious and you would rather not go through the app, write to us directly at the address above.

Deleting my account

In the app: Profile → My Hermon account → Delete my account. No password is asked — this account never had one.

Going with the account: your posts, your comments, your public profile, your photo, and your synced notes, highlights and bookmarks. What is written on your device stays there — export a backup from Profile before you leave if you want to keep it elsewhere.

Frequent questions

Do I need an account to read?

No. On opening, choose “Read without an account”. The twenty-three translations, the lexicons, the dictionary, the cross-references and the courses are already on the device. An account only carries your work to another device and lets you post in L’Assemblée.

Does the app work offline?

Entirely, for reading and study. Only the community and sync need the network.

Why is the app so large?

Because everything is inside: the texts, the lexicons, the dictionary, the hundreds of thousands of cross-references and the course artwork. That is what lets you work on a plane, in a basement, or where data is expensive. Plan to download over Wi-Fi.

Are my notes backed up?

They live on your device. Two ways to put them out of harm: turn on notebook sync from My Hermon account, or export a backup from Profile and keep it where you like. Deleting the app takes with it whatever was neither synced nor exported.

I did not receive my code

Look in your junk folder: the code arrives from an address your mail does not know yet. If nothing comes after a few minutes, ask for another one — the previous one then stops working. And if it still resists, write to us.

Where do the translations come from?

All of them are public domain or openly licensed. The app carries a Sources screen naming each text, its licence and its origin, one by one. The biblical texts belong to their respective publishers; we claim none of them.

An error in a text or a course

Tell us the exact reference and what you are reading. Those reports are the most useful ones we get, and they are fixed first.
