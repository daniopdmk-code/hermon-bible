Privacy

Hermon Bible is a Scripture study library that fits entirely on your device. The simplest thing to say is also the truest one: without an account, nothing leaves.

Who writes this page

Hermon Bible is published by PDMK (Daniel Mwamba), Calgary, Canada. For any question about your data: daniopdmk@icloud.com.

Without an account, nothing leaves

The app opens with no sign-up. The twenty translations, the lexicons, the dictionary, the cross-references and the courses are already on the device when you install it. You can read, search and annotate all of it without ever giving us a single piece of information, and without a connection.

What never leaves your device

Unless you explicitly ask for it, the following stays on your phone or tablet, and we have no access to it:

  • your notes, highlights, bookmarks and study files;
  • your sermon manuscripts;
  • your reading history and course progress;
  • your settings and the question you write on arrival;
  • recordings of your speaking exercises.

If you turn on iCloud sync, these travel through your own iCloud account, with Apple, and not through our servers.

What leaves if you open an account

A Hermon account does two things, both optional: it carries your work from one device to another, and it lets you post in the community, “L’Assemblée”. You open it with a code sent by email or SMS, or with Sign in with Apple. No password is ever created or stored.

What you post is public

In “L’Assemblée”, your posts, comments, reactions, photos and videos are visible to everyone using the app. Do not post anything you would not say out loud in a room.

Your profile photo is publicly readable. It is kept in an open store, and its address can be opened by anyone who has it, even outside the app. That is how a public profile photo normally works, but it is better to know before choosing one.

Every report is read by the team within twenty-four hours; offending content is removed and its author may be suspended. You can block someone at any time, and undo that block.

Camera, microphone and photos

The app asks for these permissions only when you choose to record a short video with a verse, or to set a profile photo. Nothing is captured without your gesture, and refusing only disables that one feature.

What we do not do

  • No tracking. The app does not follow what you do in other apps or on the web, and belongs to no advertising network.
  • No advertising, and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics. There is no analytics library in the app — and, more broadly, no third-party library at all.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, to anyone, under any circumstances.
  • We do not read your notes. They are yours.

Who hosts what

  • Supabase — accounts, profiles, and the notebook if you turn on sync.
  • Apple (CloudKit) — the community posts, and iCloud sync when you enable it.
  • Brevo — sending the email code, nothing else.
  • SMS codes are sent through our account host’s provider.

These providers act on our behalf and may process your data outside your country of residence, in particular in the United States and Canada.

How long

Your data stays as long as your account exists. It goes when you delete it. Our hosts’ technical backups may keep a trace for a few weeks before being overwritten in turn.

Your rights, and the way out

You can access, correct, export and delete your data.

To delete your account, inside the app: Profile → My Hermon account → Delete my account. No password is asked.

Going with it: your posts, your comments, your public profile, your photo, and your synced notes, highlights and bookmarks. If you signed in with Apple, the matching token is revoked with Apple. What is written on your device stays there, and you can export it from Profile before you leave.

To exercise a right another way, write to us: we answer within thirty days. Depending on your country, you may also contact your data protection authority.

Younger readers

The app is not intended for children under thirteen, and we do not knowingly collect their data. A parent who finds otherwise can write to us and we will delete the account.

If this page changes

The date at the top governs. A change that genuinely affects you will be announced in the app, not only here.
